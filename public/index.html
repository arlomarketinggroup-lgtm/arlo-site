We test extensively before going live. You hear Aria in action and approve how she sounds before she takes a single real call. If anything needs adjusting after launch — new services, updated hours, changed providers — we fix it fast, usually same day.

How long does setup take? +
Most spas are live within 48 hours of their onboarding call. We build everything from scratch — your services, providers, pricing, and hours — so it sounds exactly like your spa, not a generic bot. You just give us the info and we handle the rest.

Can it handle multiple providers with different schedules? +
Yes. Each provider gets their own calendar with their own availability. Aria asks callers for their preference and books with the right person automatically. No overlap, no double-booking, no confusion.

What happens if a caller asks something Aria doesn't know? +
Aria is trained to handle this gracefully. If she doesn't have the answer, she tells the caller that someone from your team will follow up — she never guesses or makes things up. You can also set a transfer option so complex calls go straight to a live person.

No contracts. We keep it month to month and adjust pricing based on your call volume. If something isn't working for you, you can cancel anytime. We'd rather earn your business every month than lock you in.
